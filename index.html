What we do

Three disciplines. One beast.

Hire us for one discipline or all three. Designers, artists and tech artists sitting together means fewer handoffs and less time lost between your vision and the build.

01 / DESIGN

Game Design

Systems that feel incredible in the hands, with every encounter tuned through playtesting.

  • Combat design
  • Gameplay & systems design
  • Narrative design
  • Enemy & boss AI (Behaviour Trees, EQS)
  • QA & playtest analysis
02 / ART

3D Game Art

Worlds you want to get lost in: modelled, textured and dressed so they're ready for the engine.

  • Environment art & set dressing
  • Hero & world props
  • PBR materials (Substance Painter & Designer)
  • Sculpting in ZBrush
  • Modelling in Maya & Blender
03 / TECH ART

Tech Art

The magic between art and code: shaders, VFX and tools that make your team faster.

  • Shaders & material functions
  • Niagara VFX
  • Tools & Blueprints
  • Houdini procedural pipelines
  • Performance & optimisation
Unreal Engine 5, end to end.

We don't just hand over files. We build, integrate and test inside your project.
