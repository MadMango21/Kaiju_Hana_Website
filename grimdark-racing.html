GrimdarkRacing

Warhammer 40,000 meets the kart racer. Ultramarines and World Eaters tear around a custom track in the grim darkness of the far future, with weapons built for constant aggression.

Kart racerVehicular combatWarhammer 40KUnreal Engine 5

Overview

Most kart racers lean into cartoonish, hectic fun. We wanted visceral combat and the grim darkness of the 41st millennium, an aesthetic that's the complete opposite of what the genre usually looks like.

Built by a 10-person team in Unreal Engine 5, the project balanced deep Warhammer lore with accessible racing. The goal was design that honours the source material while staying fun for players who've never heard of a Primaris Marine.

The weapons and environments draw directly from established lore. The karts are original designs that look like they belong in the universe but are uniquely ours.

Design notes

Behind the race.

Push-forward combat

Weapon design follows DOOM 2016's push-forward philosophy of constant aggression and constant engagement, which fits the pace of a kart racer naturally. Every weapon has a role in a combat-chess sandbox: the plasma gun handles ranged area damage, the chainsword is for close-up duels and the autocannon covers long-range precision. No weapon is redundant.

Combat design

Why Warhammer 40,000?

The art director and Humza are both huge 40K fans and saw a chance to inject something genuinely different into the genre. The contrast between kart-racer fun and the grim darkness of the setting was exactly the point.

Creative direction

Honouring the lore, adding our DNA

Weapons and environments needed to feel authentic to fans, so they draw directly from established lore and games. The karts were a different challenge. Instead of recreating existing vehicles, we designed originals that feel entirely at home in the universe.
